Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

"Quran Reels – Daily Sakina" is an internal tool used only by the Daily Sakina team to publish short Quran recitation videos to its own Facebook Page, Instagram, YouTube channel and TikTok account.

Data we use: access tokens for our own accounts, used only to upload and publish videos. We do not collect, store, share or sell personal data about viewers or any other users.

Storage & security: tokens are stored encrypted in GitHub Actions Secrets, accessible only to the account owner.

Data deletion: app access can be revoked at any time from Facebook, Google or TikTok settings, or by emailing obelix.agency@gmail.com.

Google API data: use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used subject to the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
